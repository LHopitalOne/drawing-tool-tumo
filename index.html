How it all started

Born from necessity during an AI+Art workshop at TUMO Center for Creative Technologies in July 2025. We - Alex (left) and Elen (crouching right) - needed a simple way for middle schoolers to share drawings as links—no downloads, no registration, no complexity. Nothing like that existed, so we built it.

What started as a workshop tool became something more: an elegant drawing platform that does exactly what you need and nothing you don't. No complicated registration process, no file clutter, no headache. Just you and the canvas.
